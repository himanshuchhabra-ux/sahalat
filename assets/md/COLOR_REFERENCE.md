(#9AEFB8)**\n- Highlight emphasis\n- Interaction feedback\n- Non-intrusive guidance\n- Micro-interactions\n\n**Warm Peach (#D69467)**\n- Important CTAs\n- Alert notifications\n- Key moments\n- Visual
