Manage frequently asked questions. Add, edit, or remove FAQs to help users find answers quickly.
How do I create an account?
To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" button on the homepage. Fill in your email address, create a secure password, and follow the verification steps. Your account will be ready to use within minutes.
What is KYC verification?
KYC (Know Your Customer) is a verification process required by law. You'll need to provide government-issued ID, proof of address, and identity verification to complete KYC. This helps us ensure account security and prevent fraud.
Is my money safe in this wallet?
Yes, your funds are secured using contemporary encryption technology. We follow industry-standard security protocols and store funds in secure, insured accounts. Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of protection to your account.
How long do transfers take?
Domestic transfers typically take 1-2 business days. International transfers may take 3-5 business days depending on the destination country and banks involved. We'll provide real-time updates on your transfer status.
What are the transaction fees?
Transaction fees vary based on the type and amount. Domestic transfers have a flat fee of $1 or 0.5% of the amount (whichever is lower). International transfers have a flat fee of $5. View detailed fee information in the Pricing section of our website.
How do I enable two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. Choose between SMS or authenticator app. Follow the verification steps and you're all set. This adds an extra security layer to your account.
Can I withdraw my funds anytime?
Yes, you can withdraw your funds anytime. However, you must have completed KYC verification. Withdrawals are processed during business hours and may take 1-2 business days depending on your bank.
What if I forget my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address and follow the recovery steps. You'll receive a secure link to reset your password. Complete the process and you can log in with your new password.
Is customer support available 24/7?
Our customer support team is available 24/7 to assist you. You can reach us via email, live chat, or support tickets. We typically respond to inquiries within 2 hours during peak hours.
Can I link multiple bank accounts?
Yes, you can link up to 5 bank accounts per user. Each account must be verified separately. Visit your account settings to manage linked accounts and set your primary account for transfers.
What are the limits on transactions?
Standard users can transfer up to $10,000 per transaction. Verified users can transfer up to $50,000. Enterprise users have custom limits. These limits reset daily and are subject to regulatory compliance.
How do I report suspicious activity?
If you notice suspicious activity, immediately contact our support team through the Help section or call our fraud hotline. Do not share your password or OTP with anyone. We take security seriously and investigate all reports.
